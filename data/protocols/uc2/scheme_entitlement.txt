[PROTOCOL: AYUSHMAN_BHARAT_PMJAY_ENTITLEMENT]
[Source: National Health Authority (NHA) Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Official Operational Guidelines]
Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) provides free health coverage of up to Rs 5,000,000 (5 Lakhs) per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization to over 12 crore poor and vulnerable families.
All empanelled public hospitals and private hospitals provide cashless and paperless treatment for NCD complications (heart procedures, stroke care, diabetic complications, kidney care).
To check eligibility:
1. Visit your nearest Ayushman Bharat Health and Wellness Centre (HWC) or public hospital with your Ration Card or Aadhaar Card.
2. An Ayushman Mitra at the hospital counter checks your family name in the PM-JAY database.
3. If eligible, an Ayushman Golden Card is issued immediately at zero cost.

[PROTOCOL: ABHA_HEALTH_ID_CARD_ENROLMENT]
[Source: Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) Guidelines]
ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) is a 14-digit unique health ID number for every Indian citizen.
ABHA card enables digital storage of all medical prescriptions, lab reports, NCD screening history, and doctor visit records across public and private hospitals.
To create an ABHA card:
- Provide your 12-digit Aadhaar number or Mobile number to your local ASHA worker or ANM at the Sub-Centre / HWC.
- Authentication is completed via instant mobile OTP.
- ABHA card generation is completely free of cost.

[PROTOCOL: FREE_NCD_MEDICINE_AND_SCREENING_AT_HWCS]
[Source: MoHFW Ayushman Bharat Health and Wellness Centre (HWC) Guidelines]
Under the National Programme for Prevention and Control of NCDs (NP-NCD):
- Free NCD Screening: All individuals aged 30 years and above are entitled to FREE annual screening for Hypertension, Diabetes, and common cancers at their local Sub-Centre / HWC.
- Free Essential NCD Medicines: Empanelled HWCs and Primary Health Centres (PHCs) provide FREE monthly supplies of anti-hypertensive drugs (Amlodipine, Telmisartan) and diabetes drugs (Metformin) under the National Essential Medicines List (NEML).
- Free Diagnostic Tests: Blood Pressure measurement, Blood Glucose testing, and basic urine tests are provided free of cost to all patients.
