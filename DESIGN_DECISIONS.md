# Engineering Design Decisions & Trade-Off Log — VDA

This document records key technical decisions, alternative approaches considered, and rationales for choices made in the Virtual Digital Assistant (VDA) project.

---

### Entry 01: Scope Selection (UC1 + UC2 Live Implementations vs. Shallow Coverage)
- **Decision**: Build Use Case 1 (NCD Care Adherence) AND Use Case 2 (Hypertension & Diabetes Lifestyle Guidance) to live production depth. Scope UC3 and UC4 as architectural designs (`UC2_UC4_DESIGN.md`).
- **Alternative Considered**: Building shallow stub implementations for all 4 use cases.
- **Rationale**: Demonstrating two fully functional, audited use cases with citable RAG protocols and zero-PII memory retention provides higher credibility than four fragile stubs.

---

### Entry 02: Rule-Based Deterministic Safety Gate — Defensible Benchmark & Adversarial Verification
- **Decision**: Implemented a rule-based regex pattern matcher (`backend/safety_gate/gate.py`) running prior to LLM execution.
- **Benchmark Performance**: **Recall: 100.00% (19/19)** | **Precision: 100.00% (21/21)** across 40 labeled benchmark samples.
- **Mixed-Signal Adversarial Test Suite**: Verified 5/5 mixed-signal adversarial test cases (`tests/test_mixed_signal_adversarial.py`) where red-flag symptoms are buried inside small talk, routine adherence questions, or normal blood sugar readings.
- **Defensible Interview Framing**: The 100.00% metric reflects performance across our 40-sample benchmark set and 5 adversarial test cases. In an interview, frame this as: *"100% on our labeled benchmark set; here is the specific adjective-order FAST stroke case we caught during iterative testing (`speech is slurred` vs `slurred speech`), and here is how we would expand the benchmark set next with continuous clinician auditing."*

---

### Entry 03: Sarvam AI Resiliency & Verified Live Failover Circuit
- **Decision**: Primary STT/TTS provider option set to Sarvam AI (Saaras ASR + Bulbul TTS) with automatic fallback wrappers.
- **Empirical Failover Proof**: Verified via `tests/test_sarvam_failover.py` simulating an HTTP 403 invalid key / network API failure. The system logged `WARNING:backend.tts.sarvam_tts:[SarvamTTS] Status 403. Falling back.` and automatically routed execution to Google Cloud / Mock TTS without breaking the live user session.

---

### Entry 04: Multi-Document PDF RAG Indexing (12 Official ICMR/WHO Guidelines)
- **Decision**: Ingested and chunked 12 official government and WHO guidelines (`ICMR-NIN 2024 Dietary Guidelines for Indians`, `ICMR Type 2 Diabetes Guidelines 2018`, `WHO Physical Activity Guidelines 2020`, `WHO HEARTS Technical Package`, `MoHFW NPCDCS Guidelines`) into 42 citable protocol chunks.
- **Rationale**: Ensures every answer generated by VDA for both UC1 and UC2 is traceable back to official public health guidelines with explicit source citation tags.

---

### Entry 05: Patient View Color Palette (#FAF5EC Paper & #B8456B ASHA Rose Accent)
- **Decision**: Patient View uses a warm light `#FAF5EC` paper background with a primary `#B8456B` rose accent.
- **Rationale**: 
  1. **Outdoor Sunlight Legibility**: Rural patients frequently use their phones outdoors under direct sunlight where dark backgrounds suffer severe mirror glare.
  2. **Cultural Grounding**: The `#B8456B` rose accent echoes the iconic sari color of India's Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHA workers).

---

### Entry 06: Ephemeral Memory Store for Zero PII Retention
- **Decision**: Store active dialogue context in RAM (`EphemeralSessionStore`) with a 30-minute TTL and explicit `DELETE /api/session/{session_id}` purge endpoint.
- **Rationale**: Fulfills Non-Negotiable Constraint #4 ("No PII retention after session end").
